AI News Summarizer
& Tamil Translator

Next-Generation Multi-Agent AI News Intelligence Platform using AutoGen, Groq, FastAPI and GNews API.

🚀 Live Project GitHub →
BUSINESS PROBLEM

Staying updated with news is time-consuming.

People often struggle to stay updated with the latest news because articles can be lengthy, information is distributed across multiple news websites, and many users prefer reading information in their native language.

Manually searching multiple sources, reading complete articles, understanding the key information and translating the content can take significant time.

AI SOLUTION

Multi-Agent AI News Intelligence Platform

Developed a multi-agent AI application that automatically retrieves the latest news, generates concise AI-powered summaries, translates the summaries into natural Tamil and presents the information through a responsive web interface.

01

News Search Agent

Retrieves relevant and up-to-date news articles using news APIs and web search.

02

Summarizer Agent

Extracts important information and generates concise AI-powered news summaries.

03

Tamil Translator Agent

Converts the AI-generated summary into natural and reader-friendly Tamil.

MULTI-AGENT WORKFLOW
