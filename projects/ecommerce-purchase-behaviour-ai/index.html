01 — BUSINESS PROBLEM

Identify customers before they become inactive.

E-commerce companies generate large amounts of customer activity and purchase data. However, identifying customers who may stop purchasing can be challenging using traditional analysis methods.

Businesses need an effective way to analyze customer behaviour and identify potential purchase continuation risks early, allowing them to take proactive retention and re-engagement actions.

02 — SOLUTION

AI-powered customer behaviour prediction.

01

Customer Input

Capture structured customer activity and purchase-related information through the web dashboard.

02

Data Processing

Process customer input and convert categorical information using the saved label encoders.

03

Random Forest

The trained Random Forest classification model analyzes the processed customer activity data.

04

AI Prediction

The application generates a purchase continuation prediction and presents the result through the dashboard.
