BUSINESS PROBLEM

Enterprise email processing is repetitive and time-consuming.

Modern organizations receive hundreds or thousands of customer emails every day, including product refund requests, delivery delays, warranty claims, order cancellations, billing questions, technical support requests and customer complaints.

Traditional email handling requires employees to manually read, classify, prioritize, understand and respond to every message.

This can lead to slow response times, inconsistent replies, human errors, difficulty following company policies, increased operational costs and poor customer experience.

AI SOLUTION

Multi-Agent AI for enterprise email operations.

The system automates the complete email processing workflow while maintaining policy compliance, response quality and human oversight.

01

Understand

AI analyzes the customer email, intent, category, urgency and sentiment.

02

Retrieve

Qdrant RAG retrieves relevant company knowledge and supporting information.

03

Validate

Company policies and business rules are applied before generating the response.

04

Automate

AI generates, reviews, formats and sends the final email with human approval when required.
